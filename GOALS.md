# Goals and constraints

Build durable research knowledge whose claims can be traced, challenged,
reproduced, superseded, and reused without losing uncertainty or provenance.

Priorities: correctness over sophistication; evidence over narrative;
falsifiability over persuasion; measurement validity before modeling;
reproducibility before optimization; minimal infrastructure before distribution.

Phase 01 delivers a JSON catalog, schema, local validator, deterministic tests,
governance, and GitHub review templates. It does not establish any market finding.

Future research may span CME/COMEX MBO, crypto derivatives, Vietnam equities and
VN30 futures, rates, credit, FX, energy, and institutional finance. Transfer across
assets is a hypothesis requiring venue, clock, contract, liquidity, and data-rights
checks; a successful implementation in one asset does not prove transferability.

Out of scope now: external repository discovery, cloning candidates, experiments,
ML, vector databases, RAG, microservices, live data, private data, and trading.
Research stays separate from automated order execution. Consequential changes
require explicit human approval.
